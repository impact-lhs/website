The Integrated Metrics for Perioperative Analytics and Clinical Tracking system connects routinely collected healthcare data around clinically meaningful episodes and time, creating consistent views of patients, diseases and populations.

Explore the approach

01 / Purpose

A network for learning
from care.

IMPACT is a network of people and organisations working to make high-quality clinical information accessible to the people who can use it to improve care.

We develop shared approaches to clinical data and learning, designed for the people who make change, not only those who ask for information.

People improve healthcare, not data.

02 / Evidence in context

Evidence matters.
Context matters too.

Evidence-based medicine provides an essential foundation for clinical practice. But evidence generated in defined populations must ultimately be interpreted and applied to individual patients and local health systems.

IMPACT connects published evidence with reliable information about local populations, clinical practice and outcomes.

What does the evidence mean here, for the patients we actually care for?

04 / From data to action

Data does not improve care.
People do.

  1. StructureConsistent definitions make observations comparable.
  2. ConnectClinical knowledge establishes meaningful relationships.
  3. LearnPatients, clinicians, analysts and researchers interpret the evidence.
  4. ImproveLearning returns to care, where people can act on it.

05 / Learning from care

Make learning part
of everyday healthcare.

Routine clinical information can help identify variation, inequalities, associations and unexpected outcomes, generating questions for research and opportunities for improvement.

IMPACT connects clinical practice, informatics, research, improvement and education so that learning can return to care.

What do the data show, how reliable are they, and what should we do in response?
