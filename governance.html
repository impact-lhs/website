Governance

Founding Governing Board

The IMPACT Founding Governing Board provides strategic oversight and safeguards the collaborative's development as a clinically led, multi-institutional learning health system.

Membership is designed to balance institutional, clinical, analytical, research, implementation and public perspectives, without allowing any single organisation or professional group to determine IMPACT's direction.
